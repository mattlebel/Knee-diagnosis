    Running while it settles

    Pain-monitoring rule

    • Pain up to 3 / 10 during exercise or a run is acceptable.
    • It should settle within 24 hours and not be worse the next morning.
    • If it builds week to week, drop the load back a step.

    Strength routine

    • Do the exercises 3–4 times a week, both legs, sore side first.
    • Slow and controlled: about 3 seconds on the lowering part.
    • Add load or reps when the last set feels easy for two sessions in a row.

    See a physio or doctor if

    • The knee locks, gives way, or swells quickly.
    • You can't put weight on it, or pain wakes you at night.
    • Numbness, tingling or a weak, slapping foot.
    • Calf swelling, warmth or redness, or fever.
    • No clear improvement after 2–3 weeks.

    This is a self-assessment aid built on common clinical patterns, not a diagnosis. Several of these conditions overlap, and a hands-on exam by a sports physio is the fastest way to confirm what's going on. Your marks are saved only in this browser.
